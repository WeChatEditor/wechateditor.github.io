# 网站图标素材

使用内置 imagegen 生成，源图为 `favicon-source.png`。派生文件位于 `apps/frontend/public/`：16/32/48/64px 多尺寸 ICO、32px 与192px PNG、180px Apple 图标。源图按原比例缩小，不裁切；保留透明圆角。

## 最终提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production website favicon for 桀士排版·公众号助手, a clean Chinese article typesetting editor. Design ONE flat, highly simplified logo: a solid royal blue (#2563EB) rounded square tile filling almost the entire square canvas, with a bold white capital J subtly integrated into a white outlined document page. Two short thick white horizontal typesetting lines on the upper-right side, with the J descending and curling left at the bottom. Strong coherent silhouette, generous internal space, center composition, very clear at 16px and 32px. Square image, smooth crisp edges, flat vector-like shapes. Background outside rounded tile truly transparent. No words, no Chinese characters, no extra letters, no pencil, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no 3D, no mockup, no border outside the tile. The tile should occupy 94 percent of canvas width with a tiny transparent margin; white mark occupies 65 percent. Elegant editorial productivity brand, blue and white only.
